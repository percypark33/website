A little more about Sweet Snow

What is bingsu?

Bingsu, also spelled bingsoo, is a Korean shaved ice dessert. At Sweet Snow, our milk base is shaved into soft snow and layered with fruit, syrups and toppings.

Where is Sweet Snow?

Find us inside the QT Golden Market Place food court at 9772 Garden Grove Blvd, Garden Grove, CA 92844. Look for the blue Sweet Snow sign.

Can I order pickup or delivery?

Use Order online to see today’s pickup menu and availability. Delivery is available through our DoorDash link.

What are croffles and mini taiyaki?

A croffle is croissant dough cooked in a waffle maker. Our mini taiyaki are small filled, fish-shaped pastries, baked to order and served as seven pieces in a small kraft paper bag.
