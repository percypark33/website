What Is Snowflake Bingsu?

Korean shaved milk ice, soft as freshly fallen snow.

Light, Fluffy, Melt-In-Your-Mouth

Bingsu is a Korean shaved ice dessert, also spelled bingsoo. Snowflake bingsu takes its name from the delicate flakes that resemble snow. Made with a milk base, this modern style has a soft, creamy texture that melts on your tongue.

At Sweet Snow, we use only milk for our snow, shaved on an authentic snowflake machine from Korea. Each flavor brings its own combination of fruit, house-made syrups and toppings. Our full-size bowls are made to share between two or three people.

From Ice To Milk Snow

Korea has a long tradition of chilled ice desserts. Early ice-and-fruit preparations were different from the bingsu bowls we know today. Over time, shaved ice topped with sweet red bean became a familiar favorite known as patbingsu.

The milk-snow style is a more recent chapter. Snowflake-style bingsu gained popularity during the 2000s, and shaved-milk versions became mainstream in Korea in the early-to-mid 2010s as dessert cafés expanded. Today, bowls range from red bean and roasted soybean powder to fresh fruit and tea flavors.
